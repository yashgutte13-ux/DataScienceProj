### 2. Detailed Data Quality Dimensions Evaluated
#### A. Completeness (Missing Value Remediation)
- **Customer Zip Codes**: Imputed missing postal codes based on modal distribution per city/state.
- **Product Categories**: Missing category values filled with `'unclassified_general'` fallback.
- **Logistics Delivery Dates**: Missing customer delivery dates for non-delivered items (`shipped`, `processing`, `canceled`) appropriately flagged without schema truncation.

#### B. Uniqueness (Deduplication)
- Exact customer duplicates generated by repeated registrations were detected and eliminated based on `customer_id` primary key constraint.
- Double-processed payment records (duplicate `order_id` + `payment_sequential`) were purged prior to Star Schema loading.

#### C. Validity & Format Standardization
- **Date Formats**: Mixed datetime formatting (ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` vs European `DD/MM/YYYY`) parsed and converted into uniform ISO-8601 standard timestamps.
- **Casing Consistency**: City and state names standardized into canonical lowercase trimmed strings to enable accurate geographic joins.
- **Negative Price / Value Elimination**: Negative transactions filtered or corrected to protect accounting integrity.

#### D. Outlier Detection & Handling (IQR Method)
- Extreme product weight entry outliers (e.g. 999,999g entry error) identified using 1.5 × IQR thresholds and capped at the 99th percentile.
- Order price extremes validated against order payment aggregations to guarantee referential consistency.
